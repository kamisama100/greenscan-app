📥 How to Install

  1. Tap Download for Android above (or scan the QR code).
  2. When the file finishes, tap it to open. Your phone may warn that it's from an unknown source.
  3. Tap Settings on that warning, then turn on “Allow from this source” (or “Install unknown apps”).
  4. Go back and tap Install.
  5. Open GreenScan and sign up using your school email.
⚠️ The “unknown source” warning is normal for apps installed outside the Play Store — GreenScan is safe. You can turn the setting back off after installing.

✅ What You Need

  • An Android phone (version 7.0 or newer)
  • Your University of Mindanao school email to sign up
  • Internet connection to identify plants (results are saved for offline viewing)
  • Camera & location permission (location tags where you found each plant)

📸 Quick Tips for Best Results

  • Photograph one plant part at a time — a single leaf, flower, fruit, or bark.
  • Don't photograph the whole plant — close-ups are more accurate.
  • Add up to 4 parts of the same plant: more parts = more accurate.
  • Use good lighting and wait for the green “Plant detected” check.
